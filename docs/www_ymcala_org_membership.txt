As a member, you get access to Y centers across the country. Here’s how to make the most of our nationwide membership program:
Call Before You Visit
: Contact the Y you plan to visit in advance to ensure it is one of the more than 2,600 Ys in the U.S. participating in the nationwide membership program.
Ask About Benefits:
Each Y may have different membership benefits than the YMCA of Metropolitan Los Angeles. Reach out or visit their website to see what they have in store.
Activate Membership:
Only active members have access to this perk. Is your membership on hold? Email
[email protected]
to reactivate your membership today!
Bring the Essentials:
Bring your photo ID and plan to sign a liability waiver when you visit other Ys. If you’re under 18, a parent or guardian will need to sign the waiver for you.
Heads up! To keep your nationwide membership active, at least half of your monthly visits have to be to your Home Y—the one where you originally signed up and pay dues. Usage is tracked individually, not by household. If you’re using the LA Y app to reserve classes or facilities, you’ll need to register separately and renew every 30 days. You can book up to a day in advance!
All Ys have the right to restrict or revoke nationwide membership privileges. Registered sex offenders cannot participate.

Cost should never be a barrier to finding the support you need. As a leading nonprofit organization, we offer financial assistance for most of our programs and memberships.
We are a nonprofit organization driven by our longstanding goal of seeing communities prosper through programs that promote youth development, healthy living, and social responsibility. Guided by our core values of caring, honesty, respect, and responsibility, the Y is dedicated to giving people of all ages, backgrounds, and walks of life the opportunity to reach their full potential with dignity.
Eligibility:
Financial assistance is available to anyone based on family income, number of household members, and the availability of Y funds. It is provided fairly and openly on a first-come, first-served basis.
